FAQ

Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't here, ask us on WhatsApp.

Are sessions one-on-one?

Yes. Every personal training and Pilates session is private: one client, one coach. Only one session runs in the studio at a time.

I've never trained before. Is that okay?

Yes. Plenty of our clients start from zero. We begin with the basics, teach technique properly and progress at a pace that suits you.

How does personal training work?

After a short consultation, your coach writes a programme for your goal. In each session you train together: your coach sets up the exercises, corrects your technique and tracks your progress so the plan keeps moving forward.

Can you help with muscle gain or fat loss?

Yes. Strength training is the base for both goals. We build your programme on it, add conditioning work and update the plan as you progress.

Are Pilates sessions private too?

Yes. Pilates sessions are one-on-one, on the Reformer and Cadillac.

Do you coach in English?

Yes. Sessions are available in English or Turkish.

How does the first consultation work?

It's a short conversation about your goals, training background, any injuries and when you're available. Then we recommend how to start.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the package you choose. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you the current options.

Where is the studio?

Sıraselviler Caddesi No: 2, Kuloğlu, Beyoğlu, Istanbul. It's a short walk from Taksim Square and the centre of Cihangir.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp or call. Sessions are by appointment, Monday to Friday 08:00–22:00 and Saturday 08:00–18:00.
