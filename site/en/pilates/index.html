Equipment

Reformer and Cadillac

Sessions use the studio's two Pilates apparatus. We choose which to use based on your goal.

Reformer

A sliding carriage with spring resistance. Controlled movements on the Reformer build core strength, posture and flexibility.

Cadillac

A versatile table fitted with bars and springs. It allows assisted stretching, strength and movement-control work in many positions.

What it's for

What we work on in private Pilates

In a 1-on-1 session, exercises are chosen for your body and your goal, and your instructor watches the control of every movement.

  • Posture and alignment
  • Core strength and control
  • Mobility and flexibility
  • Balance and coordination
  • Controlled work that complements strength training

Better together

Pilates and strength training, together

At QUAKE, Pilates and personal training are under one roof. If you like, we combine both in one weekly plan: strength sessions with weights, Pilates sessions for control and mobility.

FAQ

Pilates questions

If your question isn't here, ask us on WhatsApp.

Are Pilates sessions private?

Yes. Every Pilates session is one-on-one; we don't run group classes.

I've never done Pilates. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We start with the core principles at a level that suits you, and you learn the equipment step by step.

Reformer or Cadillac?

We decide together which apparatus to use based on your goal and level.

Can I combine Pilates with personal training?

Yes. We can combine strength sessions and Pilates sessions in one weekly plan.

Are sessions available in English?

Yes. Sessions are available in English or Turkish.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the package you choose. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you the current options.
