How we work

What a session looks like

Every session is part of your programme. Your coach explains what you're doing and why, watches every set, and plans the next session around how today went.

  • Warm-up and preparation: joints and muscles prepared for the day's work.
  • Main strength work: fundamental lifts such as squats, deadlifts, presses and rows, in variations that suit your level.
  • Conditioning or accessory work: functional training, conditioning or targeted exercises, depending on your goal.
  • Tracking: your weights and reps are noted, and the programme moves forward from them.

What we work on

Whatever your goal, strength is the base.

Your programme can focus on one goal or combine several.

  • Strength

    Getting stronger in the fundamental lifts, with good technique and steadily increasing load.

  • Muscle gain

    Hypertrophy training planned with enough volume and progressive load.

  • Fat loss and body composition

    Strength and conditioning that supports fat loss while keeping your muscle.

  • Conditioning

    Conditioning and functional training so you have more in the tank, in daily life and in sport.

  • Movement quality

    Mobility, balance and control, so you move more comfortably and safely.

  • Getting back into training

    A controlled, sustainable restart after a long break.

FAQ

Personal training questions

If your question isn't here, ask us on WhatsApp.

Are sessions one-on-one?

Yes. Your coach works only with you for the whole session, and only one session runs in the studio at a time.

I've never trained before. Is that okay?

Yes. We start with the basics, teach technique step by step and only add load when you're ready for it.

How many sessions a week do you recommend?

It depends on your goal and schedule. For most people two or three sessions a week is a good start; we decide together at the consultation.

I have an injury. Can I still train?

We go through your injury history at the consultation and adapt the programme around it. Where needed, we'll ask you to check with a doctor or physiotherapist first.

Do you coach in English?

Yes. Sessions are available in English or Turkish.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the package you choose. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you the current options.

Where is the studio?
